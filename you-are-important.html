You are important.

Whatever brought you here — a cup of tea, a candle, a small handmade thing — I want you to know it wasn't made just to be sold. It was made with you in mind, before I even knew who you'd be.

I know what it is to have very little, and I know what it means to be given something with real love behind it. That's what I want every person who touches Mashallah Arts to feel: cared for, seen, and never just a transaction.

So if this tea warms you on a hard day, if this candle sits with you in a quiet moment, if this handmade piece makes you feel a little less alone — that is the whole point. That was always the point.

Thank you for being here. You are important, and you are loved.

— Nargis, Mashallah Arts
