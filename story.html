Our Story

From India, with love

Nargis Salmani Forber-Pratt has always been an artist. Long before there was a business, a name, or a website, there was a woman who noticed beauty everywhere — in the way tea steeps, in the flicker of a flame, in the small, careful things made by hand — and who couldn't help but make something of it.

She grew up in India in a family that had very little. She knew what it meant to go without, and she also knew what it meant to be given something with real love behind it, even when there wasn't much to give. That is the thread that runs through everything she has made since: scarcity and generosity, held together.

Coming to America meant starting over — a new country, a new rhythm of daily life, far from everything familiar. It asked for patience, faith, and a lot of quiet determination. Nargis built a home here, and a family: a husband, a son, and a daughter, who fill her life and who show up, one way or another, in everything she creates. Being a wife and mother has never been separate from being an artist for her — it's all the same devotion, pointed in different directions.

Nargis with her family — the people woven into every piece of Mashallah Arts, and the reason it exists at all.

Faith and beauty, poured into her hands

Every hardship Nargis has come through, and every quiet success along the way, has become part of her art rather than something set aside from it. Her faith and her sense of beauty aren't kept separate from the tea she blends or the candles she pours — they're infused into them. That's the whole idea behind Mashallah Arts: nothing here is meant to sit behind glass. It's made to be used, gifted, and lived with.

The name itself is an expression of gratitude — a way of saying "this is a gift, and we're thankful for it." Mashallah Arts is Nargis's way of making that spirit accessible to anyone who wants a little of it in their own home: a cup of tea that feels like it was made just for you, a candle lit on an ordinary evening, a handmade piece that carries a story in it.

Mashallah Arts is registered as a small business in the State of Missouri, based in Ballwin — but it started, and stays, deeply personal. Every order is still made by Nargis herself, with her family close by, and every sale helps a woman who came from very little keep building something entirely her own.

This page is a first draft of Nargis's story — more of her own words and details are on the way, and this will grow to reflect them.
