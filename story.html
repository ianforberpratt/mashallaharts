Our Story

From India, with love

Photo of Nargis coming soon

Nargis Salmani Forber-Pratt grew up in India in a family that had very little. She knew what it meant to go without — and she also knew what it meant to be given something with real love behind it, even when there wasn't much to give. Those two things, scarcity and generosity held together, shaped who she became.

When Nargis came to America, she was starting over in every sense — a new country, a new language of daily life, far from everything familiar. Building a life here took patience, faith, and a lot of quiet determination. Along the way, she never let go of the small rituals that reminded her of home: brewing tea the way she was taught, working with her hands, and finding beauty in simple, handmade things.

Mashallah Arts grew out of that. The name itself is an expression of gratitude — a way of saying "this is a gift, and we're thankful for it." That's the spirit behind everything Nargis makes: tea blends meant to warm you the way they warmed her, hand-poured candles made slowly and with care, and handmade goods that carry a piece of her story in every one.

Today, Mashallah Arts is registered as a small business in the State of Missouri, based in Ballwin — but it started, and stays, deeply personal. Every order is still made by Nargis herself, and every sale helps a woman who came from very little build something entirely her own.

Thank you for taking the time to read this far, and for supporting a small, handmade, immigrant-owned business. It means more than you know.
